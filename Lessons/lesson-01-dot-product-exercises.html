Set 1 — Predict the Sign

Look at the diagram before you calculate. Predict whether the dot product is positive, negative, or zero — then compute the exact value. One of each will appear in random order.

Set 2 — Fill in the Formula

Both vectors are drawn on the grid. Fill every blank in the dot product formula, then check your answer.

Set 3 — Draw the Vectors

The grid is blank. Click on the grid to place the tip of each vector (tails are always at the origin). After placing both, compute the dot product.

Set 4 — Geometric Formula

You are given the magnitudes of two vectors and the angle between them. Use a · b = |a| |b| cos θ to compute the dot product. The value of cos θ is provided as a hint. Round to 2 decimal places where needed.

Set 5 — From Components to Angle

Use both definitions together. Given two vectors as coordinates, compute their magnitudes and dot product, then use the geometric formula to find the angle between them. Click arccos → θ to evaluate the inverse cosine once you have your answer.

Final Exercises

Seven mixed problems covering all formula types — components, geometric formula, sign prediction, 3-D, and physics. No diagrams, no hints. Fill in all seven answers, then submit to see your score.
